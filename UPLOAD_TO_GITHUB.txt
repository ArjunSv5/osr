FAST APK BUILD

1. Go to github.com and create a new repository named CrowdVibe.
2. Extract this ZIP on your PC.
3. Open the CrowdVibe_CloudBuild folder.
4. Upload ALL files and folders inside it to the repository.
   IMPORTANT: upload the hidden .github folder too.
5. Commit the files to the main branch.
6. Open the Actions tab in GitHub.
7. Open "Build Android APK".
8. Wait for the green check mark.
9. Open the completed run.
10. Under Artifacts, download "CrowdVibe-APK".
11. Extract the downloaded artifact ZIP.
12. You will get app-debug.apk.
13. Copy app-debug.apk to your Android phone and install it.

The installed app does not require internet to run.
